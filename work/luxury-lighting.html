Luxury Lighting Website

Crafting a Premium E-commerce Experience for Luxury Lighting

E-commerce Design · UI/UX Design · Brand Identity · Web Design · 2025

Introduction

What is Lumière?

Lumière is a premium e-commerce platform for luxury lighting fixtures, designed to elevate the online shopping experience for high-end interior design products. The website serves discerning homeowners, interior designers, and architects who seek exceptional craftsmanship in lighting. The goal was to create an immersive digital showroom that mirrors the elegance of a physical luxury boutique.

My Role

Lead UI/UX Designer — E-commerce Strategy, Visual Design, Prototyping

Timeline

10 Weeks

Design focus

What I focused on

01

Premium Visual Storytelling

Luxury products demand luxury presentation. I focused on creating an editorial-style layout with generous whitespace, high-quality product photography placements, and subtle animations that let each lighting fixture become the hero of its own story.

Challenges

Challenge & Design Decisions

Balancing Luxury with Usability

Premium brands often sacrifice usability for aesthetics. I focused on creating beautiful layouts that remain highly functional—ensuring customers can find, compare, and purchase products without friction.

Serving Two Audiences

The site needed to serve both direct consumers and trade professionals. I created a dual-path experience with a trade program portal while keeping the consumer journey elegant.

Reflection

What I Learned

Luxury e-commerce is about emotion, not just transactions. Working on Lumière taught me that premium online shopping experiences need to evoke the same feelings as walking into a high-end showroom—curiosity, aspiration, and confidence in craftsmanship.
